An EPUB reader with a companion who reads with you — and is not allowed to spoil the book.

No account. No server. No telemetry. Your books, your progress and your API key stay on your phone.

READER
- EPUB 2 and EPUB 3, illustrations shown where they belong
- pages or continuous scroll, both survive chapter changes
- library with collections, multi-select, and folder import (one tap for a whole library)
- full text search inside a book and across the whole library, jumping to the exact line
- bookmarks with exact position, plus manual "read up to here" / "unread from here" marks
- built-in text to speech: reads a page aloud and turns the page for you
- five themes, three fonts, text size, spoiler-safe progress
- stats: pages, this week, streak, minutes; series are grouped into collections by title

COMPANION (bring your own key)
- any OpenAI-compatible endpoint: OpenAI, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Groq, or your own HTTPS URL
- she sees the book you are reading, reacts to it, argues about characters
- SPOILER GUARD: she only receives the text you have actually read. Pages you skipped are never in her context, so she cannot leak them even if the model has seen the book during training
- LOCAL MEMORY: facts, open threads and summaries, on the device. No vector database, no embeddings, nothing leaves the phone except the model call you made
- PERSONAS: Kaori, or duplicate her card and rewrite it, or describe your own character; the builder also works without an API key
- LOOKS THINGS UP: paste a URL into the chat and she fetches that page and reads it
- SEES PICTURES: attach a photo, and illustrations from the part you have read go with the text
- HER LANGUAGE: chips in the chat (auto / EN / PL / JA) change her answer language for this conversation

TRANSLATION
- one tap in the reader translates the page you are looking at or the whole chapter, into 19 languages
- the app tells you roughly how many tokens it will cost before you spend anything
- long chapters are split into chunks, so nothing gets cut off
- results are cached on the device: the same text never costs twice, and Settings shows the size with a Clear button
- READ opens the translated chapter in the reader as its own document (pagination, page counter, TTS) while the original book stays untouched

YOUR DATA
- export/import a backup of progress, collections, bookmarks and stats (JSON)
- memories are visible and deletable one by one, or wiped at once
- API key encrypted with the Android Keystore, Android backup disabled

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO
- no cloud, no sync, no account
- no bundled model: your key, your bill
- no DRM: plain EPUB 2/3 only (no PDF, MOBI, AZW3, CBZ)
- no speech input, no voice chat (TTS only reads aloud)
- translation is per page or per chapter on request, not a whole-book batch

GOOD TO KNOW
- you need your own API key; the app itself is free and open source (MIT)
- no books are shipped with the app
- everything runs locally; there is no KaoriReader server
