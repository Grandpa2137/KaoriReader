0.2.1

- Progress, collections, bookmarks and
  translations are written atomically: a crash
  cannot leave a half written or empty file,
  and a damaged file is set aside instead of
  being overwritten.
- Big EPUB chapters, covers and attached
  photos are read with a size cap.
- A corrupt backup reports a clear error.
- "Mark unread" says openly that it stops
  future text being sent, but does not erase
  what she has already read.
- 39 unit tests, up from 16.
