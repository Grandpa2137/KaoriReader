0.2.3

- The chat runs with the model's own reasoning on (DeepSeek), so
  invented hours and numbers are rarer.

  time maths herself.

  message, and the microphone follows it.

  172.16-31.x, *.local); public http is refused, Settings warns.
- Stats and memory writes serialised.
