0.2.5

- Illustrations now follow the read ranges like the text does: an
  image from a skipped chapter or from a page below you is never
  sent.
- Pasted links are fetched over https only, never from your own
  network, so she cannot read your router or NAS.
- Clock moved to the end of the prompt: the long book text stays
  cacheable, so chats cost less.
- A short "ok" no longer resets her language: when it is unclear she
  keeps the language of your previous message.
