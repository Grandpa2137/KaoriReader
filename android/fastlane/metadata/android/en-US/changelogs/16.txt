0.2.6

- No behaviour change. One function (ReadRanges.imagesVisible) now
  decides which illustrations the companion receives, using exactly
  the rule the text already used: guard off means the whole book,
  read ranges mean only the images inside them, and a book from
  before ranges existed falls back to the chapter you are in.
- Four new tests cover those three cases plus "ranges win over the
  fallback", so text and pictures cannot drift apart again.
