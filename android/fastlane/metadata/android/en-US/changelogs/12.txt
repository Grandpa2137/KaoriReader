0.2.2

- Microphone in the chat works again: it no longer asks for a
  permission the app does not declare (it never needed it, the
  system speech app does the recording).
- Writes are flushed to disk before the swap, so a power loss
  cannot leave an empty file.
- A damaged data file is restored from its .bak copy instead of
  showing an empty library.
- Progress saves are serialised, so two quick saves cannot
  overwrite each other.
- 45 unit tests, up from 39.
