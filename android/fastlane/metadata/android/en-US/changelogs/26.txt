0.2.16

- Settings -> MODEL: "Chat memory" lets you pick how much of the
  conversation is sent (16k / 48k / 96k / 160k characters). Default
  stays at 16k; more means she forgets less and every request is
  bigger.
- If a provider refuses a request because the history does not fit in
  the model's context, the app retries with half of it instead of
  showing an error.
